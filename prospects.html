How to read this

Rates come from your Profile Buckets history: every past player with that position and profile type. Use the Safe / Upside switch to change what the odds mean. Safe = at least one 12+ PPG season (QB 16+). Upside = a hit: 14+ PPG for WRs and TEs, 15+ for RBs, 20+ for QBs. The bars always show the full split.

Each prospect's odds use his tier + type (e.g., Upside Hog WR) from the second chart. When fewer than 5 past players share that exact combination, the odds fall back to every past player in that tier at his position (tier matters more than type) and say so. Every rate shows its sample size (n).

These are loose. Tier matters more than type: a Hog WR in the Elite tier hit 78%, but a Clogger-tier Hog hit 9%. Expect a prospect's odds to move whenever his tier changes.

Tier follows the projected pick. Your Profile Buckets tie tier to draft round: Round 4+ is always Clogger, Round 3 is Depth, Round 2 is Upside or Depth, and in Round 1 the pick decides Elite or Upside. When a prospect's projected pick doesn't fit his board tier, the page moves him to the nearest tier that does and shows his board tier under it. Tap a projected pick to change it: tier, bucket odds, comps and draft-capital odds update as you go. Type never changes. Edits stay in this browser only.
