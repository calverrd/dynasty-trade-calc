The question: early in his rookie year, is this RB on track for a 12+ PPR PPG season someday? For RBs, volume is nearly everything.

What we learned

  • Usage adds a lot to draft slot. Draft slot alone separates hits from misses fairly well, but adding early usage improves it clearly in leave-one-season-out tests. Day 3 and undrafted backs with 16+ usage hit 69% of the time vs 7% for those under 8.
  • No BUY tag. For WRs, high usage with low points is a buy signal. For RBs it isn't: high-usage backs who scored less than expected hit a bit less often, not more. Points per game is about as predictive as usage for RBs.
  • Games 1–8 is the most accurate view. Last 4 games is useful for spotting a mid-season takeover, but it's the least predictive of the three, so its tiers hit less often.
  • BF% (backfield share) is his carries + 2.5×targets as a share of all his team's RB work in those games. It isn't part of Usage, but in Games 1–4 it predicted slightly better than Usage (it strips out team pace and game script). 60%+ (green) is a lead back. Its edge fades by Games 1–8.
  • Tested and rejected: rushing EPA per carry, yards per carry and rushing first downs per carry (no gain once volume and draft slot are known), and peak 4-game rolling usage.
  • Zero-touch offensive games count. Counting games where he played offense but didn't touch the ball predicted slightly better than counting only games with a touch. A back who's on the field and not getting the ball is a real signal.

Tiers and tags

Career tiers use completed seasons of 8+ games: green = a 15+ PPG season, yellow = Starter (best season 12–14.9), red = never 12, gray = 2025–26 rookie who hasn't hit yet.

MISSED TIME Drafted, touched the ball in 2 or fewer of his team's first 4 games. Round 1–3 RBs like this hit 12+ PPG 47% of the time (vs 74%) and 15+ only 11% (vs 57%), from 19 players. A caution, not a red flag like on the WR and TE tabs: nearly half still became starters, but the ceiling drops.

Best filter combinations

2015–2024 rookies who played. Hit = a 12+ PPG season (15+ in brackets). Baseline: 33% (20%).

Buy

  • Games 1–8 · Usage 20+: 96% (78%), 23 players. The strongest signal on the site.
  • Games 1–4 · Usage 14+ · Round 1, 2 or 3: 90% (65%), 31 players. Early pick plus early work is close to a sure thing.
  • Games 1–4 · Usage 16+ · Day 3 or undrafted: 69% (31%), 13 players. The cheap way in; Day 3 backs under 8 usage hit 7%.

Be careful

  • Last 4 games · Usage 20+: 84% (57%) overall, but only 3 of 7 when his Games 1–8 usage was under 14. A late surge is often an injury fill-in; check whether the starter is coming back before paying for it.

Sell or avoid

  • Games 1–4 · Usage under 14 · Round 1, 2 or 3: 43%. Draft capital alone isn't enough.
  • MISSED TIME · Round 1–3: 15+ PPG only 11% of the time. Sell the name value.
