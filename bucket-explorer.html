How to use this tab
  • What a bucket is: a tier (Cornerstone, Elite, Upside, Depth, Clogger) plus a player type for his position (for example Hog vs Runt WRs, Workhorse vs Committee RBs). Every past player is placed in one, so each bucket shows how players like him turned out.
  • Read the matrix: every bucket's hit rate, its rate at each PPG mark, and its repeaters, colored red (0%) to yellow to green (100%). Repeater % is how often a player who hit once did it again, which tells you how sticky that profile's success is.
  • Open a bucket: tap its name for the detail view (stat tiles, hit seasons, results by era, current rookies, a sortable player list), or tap any number to see the players behind it.
  • Draft class: pick a range on the matrix and every rate is recomputed for just those classes; a bucket opened from there lists only those classes too.
  • Find a player: use the search bar at the top for his range of outcomes, or type his name in the matrix search to see his bucket's row.
  • With your league loaded, players in the detail view show who owns them and an Offer button that opens the trade calculator with a fair package.

Bucket matrix

Every bucket side by side: hit rate, the rate at each PPG mark, and how often hitters did it again. Two kinds of repeaters, kept apart: 12+ repeaters (of players with a 12+ season, the share with 2+) and hit-mark repeaters (QB 20+, RB 15+, WR 14+), which is the Repeater % in each bucket's detail. TE buckets use the 12+ column, the TE hit mark. Both leave out the newest class's one-season hitters, who can't have repeated yet. Tap a bucket to open it, or any number for the players behind it. Search finds buckets or players (with your league's owner and an Offer button), and the draft-class filter recomputes every rate for just those classes.
