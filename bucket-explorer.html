How to use this tab
  • What a bucket is: a tier (Cornerstone, Elite, Upside, Depth, Clogger) plus a player type for his position (for example Hog vs Runt WRs, Workhorse vs Committee RBs). Every past player is placed in one, so each bucket shows how players like him turned out.
  • Read the bars: green = Hit, amber = Starter, red = Bust, using the thresholds in the color key below. Repeater % is how often a player who hit once did it again, which tells you how sticky that profile's success is.
  • Compare buckets: the bucket rankings chart sorts every bucket by hit rate. Tap a bucket's title for its detail view: stat tiles, hit seasons, results by era, current rookies, and a sortable player list.
  • Find a player: use the search bar to jump to his bucket.
  • With your league loaded, players in the detail view show who owns them and an Offer button that opens the trade calculator with a fair package.
Color Key: Hit: QB 20+ · RB 15+ · WR 14+ · TE 12+ PPG Starter: QB 16+ · RB/WR 12+ PPG (TE: hit or bust only) Bust: 0 Starter seasons (TE: 0 seasons at 12+) (2006–2025) 2026 Rookie: Pending NFL sample ↻ Repeater %: Of players who reached the mark once (QB 20+, RB 15+, WR 14+, TE 14+), % who reached it in 2+ seasons
