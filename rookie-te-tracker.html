The question: is this rookie TE on track for a 12+ PPG season (1.5 PPR TE premium) someday? TEs develop slowly, so this tab leans on Games 1–8 and the full rookie season more than the first four games.

Usage score

Per game played in his team's first 4 games: targets + 10 × offensive snap share. A full-time TE on 80% of snaps with 5 targets scores 13. A game counts if he played an offensive snap or drew a target.

What we learned

  • Draft capital matters most for TEs. Draft slot alone predicts hits about as well as anything, and Games 1–4 usage adds nothing on top of it. Gemini's warning that four weeks is too noisy for TEs held up, so this tab starts at Games 1–8 (there's no Games 1–4 view).
  • Games 1–8 and the full rookie season do add to draft slot, mainly through snap share. A TE who's on the field is earning trust even before the targets come. The gain is modest: about +0.04 AUC, clearly significant for the full season and borderline for Games 1–8.
  • Other formulas didn't beat it. The WR usage formula, receptions, yards, first downs, targets per team pass attempt and points per game all did the same or worse.
  • Tested and rejected: building draft slot into the score (keep it as a filter instead) and Games 1–8 receiving yards as a way to spot future 17+ TEs. Trey McBride averaged 3 yards a game early and became the best TE in football.

Tiers and tags

Career tiers come from your Hit Rates sheet (your scoring) when the player is in it; otherwise they use completed nflverse seasons of 8+ games, scored PPR + 0.5 per catch: any 12+ PPG season is a hit, shown in three shades of green by best season: dark = 17+ (Elite, the top-3 TE line in recent years; only 6 rookies from 2015–2024 got there), medium = 14–16.9, light = 12–13.9. red = never 12 (bust), gray = 2025–26 rookie who hasn't hit yet.

MISSED TIME Drafted, played 2 or fewer of his team's first 4 games. Round 1–3 TEs like this hit 0 of 13 times, vs 46% for the rest (the same 13 players under the 4-game and 8-game rules). The strongest red flag on any tab.

CHEAP / PRICEY Model vs market. Under each unsettled rookie's market value (the calculator's Blend of KTC and FantasyCalc by default, in your league's format; the flags below still compare against KTC, the price the rule was backtested on) is his odds of a 12+ PPG season, from his usage and draft slot in the current view (the two inputs the backtests validated; refit every week). Only the current rookie class gets flags, and only with 2+ games in the view. Among them, CHEAP means KTC ranks him well below where his odds rank him (by at least a fifth of the list) and his value is at least 500 points (or 15%) below what a player at his odds rank is worth; PRICEY is the reverse. Older unsettled players show a "Y1 baseline" (odds from their rookie usage) with no flag, since the market also prices everything since then. No CHEAP / PRICEY flags on this tab: the market backtest only found an edge in the Games 1–4 window, which the TE tab doesn't use, and by Games 1–8 the market has caught up. The odds line is still shown for comparison.

Best filter combinations

2015–2024 rookies who played. Hit = a 12+ PPG season (17+ in brackets). Baseline: 16% (4%).

Buy

  • Games 1–8 · Usage 9+ · Round 1 or 2: 73% (18%), 11 players. The best TE signal we found.
  • Games 1–8 · Usage 9+ · Round 4 or later: 2 of 3 hit. Rare, but worth a cheap bid when it shows up.

Hold

  • Round 1 or 2 · Games 1–8 usage under 9: still 33%. Early-round TEs who start slow aren't dead; TEs often break out in year 2 or 3.

Sell or avoid

  • MISSED TIME · Round 1–3: 0 of 13 hit.
  • Round 4 or later · Games 1–8 usage under 6: 2 of 84 hit.

Workflow: switch to Games 1–8 (or Season to date late in the year), pick the current class, set Owner to "On other teams", filter Usage 9+, then check draft round and KTC before tapping Offer.

Comps, market value and owners: tap a name for the 5 most similar past rookies by usage and draft slot. The market column shows the calculator's value (Blend by default; KTC and FantasyCalc on hover); with a league loaded, the Owner column shows who has him and an Offer button.

Games 1–8: while in Year 2, tap Games 1–8 to use the first 8 games of his second season instead. Treat it as a read on this season: it predicts Year 2 itself a little better than Games 1–4 (mostly RBs' 15+ odds), but not Year 3 and beyond. The hit-rate lists below use Games 1–4.
