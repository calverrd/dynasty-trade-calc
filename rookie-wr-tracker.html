Per game played in Weeks 1–4: 2.2×targets + 1.0×catches + 0.12×yards + 0.5×TDs. Targets count most and TDs barely count, because four games of touchdowns is mostly luck. Draft capital is left out on purpose.

What we learned

Weeks 1–4 vs. Weeks 1–8: start with Weeks 1–4 to act early, then switch to Weeks 1–8 once it's available. It's slightly more accurate, and it catches role changes: of 12 rookies whose usage dropped a tier between Week 4 and Week 8, none hit.

  • Early role beats draft slot. Among Round 1–3 picks, the usage score predicts hits just as well with or without draft capital, while draft slot alone barely separates hits from misses.
  • High usage overrides a weak profile. Depth-bucket WRs with 15+ usage hit 19 of 22 times, about as often as Elite ones. Cloggers with 15+ hit 7 of 10.
  • The bucket matters most when usage is low. Upside WRs who start slow still hit about 4 in 10 times. Slow-starting Depth and Clogger WRs hit about 1 in 7.
  • Missing early games is a red flag, not an excuse. Round 1–3 picks who missed 2+ of the first 4 games hit only 5 of 35 times (14%), about the same as healthy players with small roles. Mike Williams and Jameson Williams are the exceptions, not the rule.

Tags

  • BUY Role ahead of production: usage 15+ but PPG under 10 and well below what that usage usually produces (bad TD luck or a slow QB). Historically 13 of 21 hit, against about 7 for rookies with the same early PPG. BUY means the market, which prices on points, is likely too low. It doesn't mean he'll beat other rookies with the same usage and draft slot; check his comps for that.
  • MISSED TIME Drafted, played 2 or fewer of the first 4 games. His score is unreliable, and history says lower your expectations (14% hit rate for Round 1–3 picks).

No Sell tag: none of the sell rules we tested held up. Late picks scoring 8+ PPG early looked promising over Weeks 1–4, but it was only 8 players, it disappeared over Weeks 1–8, and it would have flagged Puka Nacua.

Comps and KTC: tap a player's name to see the 5 most similar past rookies by usage and draft slot, and how they turned out. The KTC column shows live KeepTradeCut superflex value and rank in class. High usage with a low KTC rank in class is where the buy-lows are.

Quick read: Start with the BUY filter for the current class, then sort by Usage. A high pick with MISSED TIME and a low score is a real risk, not a guaranteed bounce-back.
