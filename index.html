What a rookie pick usually becomes

2015–2023 rookie QBs, WRs, RBs and TEs in a 12-team superflex draft order. There's no historical rookie ADP here, so the order is NFL draft slot with RBs moved up 18 picks (fantasy drafts take Round 2 RBs like Breece Hall and Jonathan Taylor in the 1st). That puts about two QBs in each class's top four. Elite = QB 20+, WR 14+, RB 15+, TE 17+ PPG in a completed season; Starter = QB 16+, others 12+; Bust = neither. The same odds show under every pick in the trade calculator. Samples are small: about 36 players in each 1st-round group (9 classes × 4 picks), 54 in each 2nd-round group. Raw, picks 5–8 hit 12+ slightly less often than 9–12 (64% vs 67%, a two-player difference), so those two are averaged to 65% to keep the odds from rising later in the round; their Elite rates are left as measured.

💡 Player Trade Idea Finder

Pick any player to generate fair 1-for-1, player + pick, and 2-for-1 upgrade offers using live league rosters. When you're shopping your own player, partners whose needs fit yours rank higher. To go after a specific player, tap Offer next to him on the Rookie WR, RB or TE tabs, in a Profile Bucket, or in his player card.

How to use this tab
  • Draft order: the strip projects next year's rookie draft. Choose whether your league orders it by standings or by Max PF; it's saved per league and sets every pick's Early / Mid / Late value and odds.
  • Sell windows on your roster: your starters at the age where production usually drops, with the contenders weakest at that position as likely buyers.
  • Power rankings: click any column to sort. Position columns rank each team's value at that spot; Fit scores how well a team's surpluses match your needs; Max PF and Win Now show who's actually strong this season.
  • Find a partner: filter to Contenders, Rebuilders or teams with next year's 1st, then tap Trade → to open the calculator against that team.
  • Playoff odds: simulates the rest of the regular season. Bubble teams (25–75%) are the likeliest buyers for veterans; unlucky teams with a strong lineup but poor odds may sell theirs.
  • Team timeline: ages every team's Win Now lineup 1 and 2 years forward on historical production curves, to show whose window is closing and who is a year away (current rosters only).
  • Pick ownership: who holds extra or missing picks for the next three drafts.
  • Handcuffs: backups for your RB1s held elsewhere, and backups you hold that another team's RB1 needs (easy throw-ins).
  • Trade history at the bottom grades every trade in the league at today's values, so you can see who tends to win deals.

📋 Buys & sells — one list

Only signals that held up in our backtests, each with the player's odds: rookies' chance of a 12+ / 14+ PPG season (WR & RB in Years 1–3, TE any season), veterans' chance of 12+ PPG again next season. Buys are on other rosters or free agents, sells are on your roster.

What each signal is worth (backtests)
  • WR BUY (role ahead of points: usage 15+, PPG under 10): 13 of 21 historical BUYs reached 12+ PPG, against about 7 of 21 rookies with the same early PPG.
  • Y2 JUMPED (second-year usage jump): 79% of RBs, 31% of WRs, 33% of TEs went on to a 12+ season.
  • When CHEAP / PRICEY show: Weeks 5–8 only. The backtest set them at Week 5 against Week 5 prices and found no edge by Week 9; later in the season the market has priced what happened since Week 4 (Nov 26 check: 5 of the 6 flags still showing in Week 12 were contradicted by Weeks 5–11 usage).
  • PRICEY (KTC ranks him far above his odds, Weeks 1–4): the strongest market signal we have. Rerun Nov 26 on 2020–2025 rookies: all 5 PRICEY rookies under the live rule lost value against similar rookies (median −60% at 12 months); with a looser cutoff, 17 PRICEY rookies trailed by 56% at 12 months (95% range −72% to −31%).
  • CHEAP (KTC ranks him far below his odds, Weeks 1–4): worth a look, not a buy call. On average CHEAP rookies gained (+53% at 12 months), but the range runs from −29% to +253%: big hits (Puka Nacua, Chase Claypool) and busts. Nearly every CHEAP flag is a 3rd-round-or-later pick, so draft round doesn't separate them.
  • KTC + FC agree / KTC only: the CHEAP / PRICEY rule run again on FantasyCalc values. A flag that holds on both is ranked a little higher; the daily signal log will show whether consensus flags do better.
  • Ask price on SELL rows: FantasyCalc values come from completed trades and price most flagged veterans above KTC (12 of 15 on Nov 26), so the higher of the two is shown as your asking price.
  • Contender / rebuild tilt: your team's League-tab tag reorders the lists (rebuilders: aging veterans first in Sell; contenders: this-season scorers up in Buy, SELL WINDOW players down in Sell). Nothing is hidden.
  • SELL NOW / SELL WINDOW (age curve, 2015–2024): veterans with 12+ PPG at that position, age and PPG level; the % is how many posted 12+ again the next season.
  • MIXED: a player with both a buy and a sell signal (for example a WR BUY whose KTC price is already PRICEY) shows in both lists with this tag. PRICEY is the better-tested of the two.
  • Act now / Solid move / Worth a look: urgency tiers, not a score. Act now = the best-tested sell signals (PRICEY with KTC + FC agreeing, SELL NOW, SELL WINDOW when rebuilding) and, for contenders, WR BUY / Y2 JUMPED players already scoring 12+ this season. Solid move = the other WR BUY / Y2 JUMPED buys, PRICEY on KTC only, SELL WINDOW when not rebuilding. Worth a look = CHEAP and MIXED. Each row keeps its own odds and backtest numbers. The retired Sell-High / Buy-Low radars were never backtested.

How This Calculator Works

1. Automatic Live Sync (Zero AI Cost)

Every time you load the page, it automatically pulls live rosters, standings, and traded picks from the Sleeper API and dynasty values from FantasyCalc (a once-a-day cached copy, per FantasyCalc's API terms; 12-team values). All trade verdicts, repeater analyses, and trade ideas run 100% locally in your browser.

2. Multi-League & 24-Team Scaling

When a league starts 20+ QBs weekly (such as a 24-team 1-QB league), QB scarcity matches a 12-team Superflex, so it automatically switches to Superflex QB values. Draft picks are also scaled to their true overall pick range (e.g., a 24-team Late 1st = picks #17–24).

3. Value Adjustment (matched to KTC)

Like KeepTradeCut, every asset gets a hidden "raw adjustment" that grows faster than its value (≈10% of value for depth pieces, up to ≈42% for the best player). The side with the bigger raw total is giving up the better piece, so it is credited a value adjustment equal to the extra value the other side would need to add. The adjustment changes smoothly, so a tiny change in values can't swing the verdict. Blend grades the trade under KTC and under FantasyCalc separately and mixes each side's share by the blend weight, so a Blend verdict always falls between the two. The formula uses KTC's own exponents with constants fitted to KTC's published numbers. Value adj. ×1.00 = KTC; lower it to soften, 0 turns it off. The League tab shows which setting best fits your league's real trades. Deals inside the Fair ± range (default 5% of the larger side) are graded Fair. The Consensus check grades every trade under KTC, FantasyCalc and the blend at once and warns you if any of them says your side loses.

4. Hit Rate & Repeater % Model

Players are grouped into historical dynasty buckets (e.g., Upside Konami QB, Elite Alpha WR, Clogger Committee RB) to flag sell-high arbitrage opportunities where market value is high but multi-season repeater probability is low.

5. Values & Blending

KTC values come from ktc.json (updated daily by a GitHub Action). Players are matched to FantasyCalc by Sleeper ID, then name; KTC players FantasyCalc doesn't list are kept. Before blending, FantasyCalc values are rank-matched onto KTC's scale, and FC tight ends get the same TE-premium lift KTC gives that player. The Trade chart's KTC / FC column shows both as published. FantasyCalc values come from a once-a-day cached copy at 12 teams, for every league size (FantasyCalc's terms ask that responses be cached and shared; 8–14-team values differ by about 1%, at most 3%).

6. Draft Picks

Pick values come from KTC's and FantasyCalc's own pick values (Early / Mid / Late anchors), interpolated to each pick's projected slot for the next draft (e.g. 1.03) and to mid-round for later years. In leagues bigger than 12 teams a pick is priced by its overall pick number.

7. Trends & History

Click any player for his KTC value and positional-rank graphs. The daily update saves a snapshot to ktc-history.json, so the graphs fill in over time; until a week of history exists, FantasyCalc's published 30-day change is shown. The League tab grades every Sleeper trade (this season and last) at today's values.
