Why keeper trades are hard to value

In a keeper league, next year's draft is a full startup draft, not a rookie draft. A 3rd-round pick isn't a rookie lottery ticket. It's whoever is left after every team keeps its players. And a player is worth what he scores this season plus how cheaply you can keep him. This page works out both for you.

Getting started

  1. Load your league. The box at the top right holds a Sleeper league ID (the long number in your league's Sleeper URL). Paste yours and press Load. The page remembers it next time.
  2. Check the rules. Open League rules near the top and make sure the keeper settings match your league. Changes save automatically and every value updates right away.
  3. Pick a tab for what you want to do (below).

The tabs

Trade calculator

  • Pick a team for each side. Their roster and draft picks appear underneath. Tap ＋ to add something to the trade.
  • Use Search any player to add anyone, including free agents, or add a generic pick by round and Early / Mid / Late.
  • The bar at the top shows whether the deal is fair (green = within 5%, amber = within 15%, red = lopsided). It also shows how each team's starting lineup and keeper core rank changes.
  • Copy DM pitch writes a message you can paste to the other manager. It's written for Side A, so put your own team there.

Keeper board

  • Every team's roster, sorted by keeper value, with the projected keepers checked.
  • Know a manager's real plans? Check or uncheck a player to lock that decision (🔒). The draft pool and every pick value recalculate from it.
  • Keeper crunch lists teams with more good keepers than slots. They'll lose the extras for nothing, so they're the best teams to trade with. Rental sellers are losing teams holding stars they can't keep.

Draft board

  • Next year's full snake draft, with every projected keeper (green K) sitting in the pick it uses up. Traded picks show "via" the original team.
  • Picks that aren't used on a keeper show as Open pick. Turn on Show mock draft in open picks to see who might still be available at each pick (marked proj.). Those players are not on that team. Hover any cell for details.
  • Click any team name or pick to highlight all of that team's picks (others fade out). Click again to clear. Lock keepers on the Keeper board and the draft board updates.

Pick values

  • What each round is worth, plus the players expected to still be available there after keepers.
  • The ownership grid shows who has extra picks (green) or traded picks away (red) in rounds 1–9.

Player values

  • Every rostered player with how he was acquired, his next keeper cost, years left, and values. Filter to Bargain keepers or Rentals, or by team.
  • If a cost or years-left is wrong (an odd trade or commissioner ruling, say), change it in the dropdown. Your fix is saved and used everywhere.

Reading a player

The big number is trade value. Underneath it splits into:

  • Redraft: what he's worth for the rest of this season.
  • Keeper: how much more he's worth than the pick it costs to keep him, over the years he has left.
  • Cost: the round you'd give up to keep him next year.
  • Yrs left: 2, 1, or 0 (a rental you can't keep).
  • K badge: projected to be kept.

Keeper rules (default)

  • Up to 3 keepers per team, each kept up to 2 years in a row.
  • Keeping costs a pick one round earlier than where he went last year.
  • Round 10+ picks and waiver adds cost a 9th, then an 8th.
  • Round 1 picks can't be kept. Round 2 picks can be kept once, for a 1st.
  • If two keepers cost the same round, the lesser one moves up a round, unless the team owns an extra pick in that round.

Season phase

Values shift through the year. In-Season leans on this season's points, Trade Deadline blends the two, and Offseason counts only keeper value, so rentals drop to 0. It's set automatically, and you can change it in League rules.

The "this season" part also shrinks as the season goes on. It only counts the weeks left, playoffs included, so a player's value for this season fades week by week while picks hold their value.

Good to know

  • Next year's draft order is projected from current standings (worst record picks first).
  • Values update every 3 hours. Press ⟳ to refresh now.
  • Next year's rookies aren't in the values yet, so late picks may read a little low.

Player values provided by FantasyCalc. League data from the Sleeper API. FantasyCalc responses are cached in your browser for 3 hours.
